Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how WGame ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Windowlight mobile game (the "Game"). It also explains the choices available to you.

2. Information We Process

Game progress and settings

Windowlight stores level progress, star results, unlocked levels, and audio preferences locally on your device. This information is used to remember your progress and settings.

Advertising and device information

The Game may request permission through Apple's AppTrackingTransparency framework. If you grant permission, an advertising identifier may be available for advertising, measurement, and fraud-prevention purposes. If you decline, the Game will respect that system-level choice.

Advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device characteristics, operating-system information, ad impressions, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Windowlight contains integrations for Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle/Liftoff, and InMobi; the active provider may vary according to availability and remote configuration.

Network and security information

The Game contacts a configuration service to request available advertising units. That request includes the app's bundle identifier and standard network request information, such as IP address and technical headers. On supported devices, an anti-fraud and security service may process a device vendor identifier and technical security signals to help detect abuse and protect the service.

Information we do not request directly

The Game does not require an account and does not ask you to submit your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or gameplay content.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Game, save progress, and remember preferences.
  • To provide and measure advertising, including optional rewarded ads for hints.
  • To retrieve advertising configuration and maintain service reliability.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical failures.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We may share or allow the processing of information by advertising, measurement, infrastructure, and security providers only for the purposes described above. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the public, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We do not sell information that you directly provide to us. Advertising partners may use information for interest-based advertising when permitted by your device settings, consent choice, and applicable law.

5. Your Choices

  • You may deny or later change tracking permission in your device's iOS privacy settings.
  • You may reset the Game's locally stored progress by deleting the app or using applicable device controls.
  • You may choose not to watch optional rewarded advertisements; doing so only affects the related optional reward.
  • You may contact us to ask questions or make a privacy request. We may need limited information to verify and respond to the request.

6. Retention

Local progress remains on your device until the Game or its stored data is removed. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies, legal duties, and operational needs. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Windowlight is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

8. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers in accordance with applicable law.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, our providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will show a new "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the Game or another appropriate channel.
